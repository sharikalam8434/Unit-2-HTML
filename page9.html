Quotation and Citation

1. Blockquote

The blockquote element defines a section that is quoted from another source.

For 60 years, WWF has worked to help...

2. Short Quotation

The q element defines a short, inline quotation. 

    <p>
        WWF's goal is to:
        <q>Build a future where people live in harmony with nature.</q>
   </p>
WWF's goal is to: Build a future where people live in harmony with nature.

3. Abbreviation

The abbr element defines an abbreviation or acronym. 

   <p>
        The
        <abbr title="World Health Organization">WHO</abbr>
        was founded in 1948.
  </p>
The WHO was founded in 1948.

4. Contact Information

The address element defines contact information for the author or owner of a document. 

    <address>
        Written by John Doe.
        Visit us at:
        Example.com
    </address>
Written by John Doe.
Visit us at:
Example.com

5. Work Title

The cite element defines the title of a creative work. 

        <cite>The Scream</cite> by Edvard Munch.
The Scream by Edvard Munch.

6. Bi-Directional Override

The bdo element defines a bi-directional override for text direction. 

    <bdo dir="rtl">
        This text will be written from right to left
    </bdo>
This text will be written from right to left
